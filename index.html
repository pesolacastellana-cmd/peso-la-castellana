About Our Office

Committed to employment facilitation and workforce development

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) of the Municipality of La Castellana serves as a link between jobseekers, employers, government agencies, and other employment partners.

Through employment facilitation, career guidance, recruitment activities, and referrals to appropriate programs, PESO works to expand access to employment and livelihood opportunities for the people of La Castellana.
